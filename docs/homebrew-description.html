YouTube AdFree by Zucchiniii: github.com/Zucchiniii/youtube-webos

YouTube on your LG TV without ads, tuned to be fast and reliable. All options are real On/Off toggles at the top of YouTube's own Settings page, so no coloured buttons are needed.

Highlights

  • No ads: video ads, the home page banner and sponsored tiles are removed.
  • SponsorBlock: skips sponsor reads, intros, outros and reminders, shown on the progress bar.
  • No black screens: a smooth transition when opening a video, and automatic recovery if a video gets stuck loading.
  • Seamless previews: OK on a playing preview grows it into the full-screen video without stopping.
  • Remember profile: skips the "Who's watching?" screen.
  • HD thumbnails everywhere, Shorts hiding, end-screen removal, a clock and audio-only mode (blue button).

Uninstall the official YouTube app first: both use the same app ID. Installing over the original webosbrew version keeps your sign-in and settings.
